Privacy Policy

A plain account of what the prototype does with data — nothing leaves your browser — and the privacy posture intended for the production platform.

Demonstration notice

This policy is part of a frontend design prototype. In this demo, all data you enter is stored only in your own browser and never transmitted anywhere.

Data we would collect

In production: identity and contact details you provide at registration, account and transaction records generated as you use the platform, device and security events used to protect your account, and support communications.

How data would be used

To operate your multi-currency accounts, verify your identity, prevent fraud and money laundering, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations in the markets served.

Storage in this prototype

The demo stores its data in your browser’s local storage only. Clearing your browser data or using “Reset demo data” removes it completely. Nothing is sent to a server.

Security

The production platform would protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication, session controls and a full audit trail of administrative access.

Your rights

In production you would be able to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements, by contacting our privacy team.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be directed to privacy@meridian.example (demo address for the prototype).
