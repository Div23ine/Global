About

A modern platform for global money

Meridian Global Accounts is a design concept for an international multi-currency platform account — a calm, precise home for money that is earned, saved and spent across borders. This page tells the story of the product the prototype proposes, and is candid about what is real and what is demonstration.

The idea

People who earn in one currency, save in another and spend in a third have traditionally been served badly: accounts that don’t speak to each other, costs folded into exchange rates, statements that obscure rather than explain. Meridian begins from the opposite premise — that a cross-border account should be as legible as a well-set page.

The concept is a single platform account holding five currencies — NGN, USD, GBP, EUR and CHF — side by side. Each currency keeps its own balance, its own history and its own statements. Nothing is converted silently, nothing is combined by default, and a newly activated currency always begins at zero. Country-based activation, one-time verification and per-currency records are all drawn from the working prototype behind this site.

Meridian is not a bank and does not provide offshore banking. It is a platform-account concept: in production, customer funds in a platform like this would be held with regulated partner institutions, under know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering obligations in every market served. The prototype exists so that this proposition can be seen, used and judged before a single line of production code is written.

What we hold to

Four commitments that shaped every screen of the prototype.

Legibility first

Every balance, fee, status and rate is shown in plain language. When a figure is demonstration data, the interface says so — on the same line, not in the footer.

Currency discipline

Each currency stands alone: separate balances, separate histories, never auto-converted. Activating a new currency moves no money — it opens at zero, deliberately.

Security as a product

Multi-factor sign-in, device-level sessions, suspicious-activity alerts and an instant self-service freeze — presented as controls you operate, not policy you accept.

Transparent administration

Administrative access is audited, visibly bannered and structurally unable to move money. Oversight you can see is oversight you can trust.

What this prototype is

A frontend design prototype — nothing more, and nothing less. This site and the application behind it exist for design review. Every user, balance, transaction, statement and notification is demonstration data stored in your own browser; nothing you do here is transmitted anywhere. It is one of three design directions — Swiss Private Banking, Hong Kong International Fintech and Premium Global — that share identical behaviour and mock data for comparison. Use the demo controls panel to reset the data at any time.
