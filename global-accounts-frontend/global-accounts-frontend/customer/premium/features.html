Everything the account can do

Eight capabilities, each working end-to-end in this prototype. Below them, two subjects worth a closer read: how deposits are simulated, and how security and administrative access are designed.

The full set

Multi-currency accounts

Hold NGN, USD, GBP, EUR and CHF side by side. Every currency account has its own balance, history and status.

Country-based activation

The currency for your country is activated automatically after verification. The mapping is configurable.

Flexible currency activation

Activate additional currencies yourself in seconds. New accounts always start at zero — balances are never auto-converted.

Simulated deposits

Debit card, credit card where supported, payment gateway and bank transfer — with success, pending, failed, cancelled and duplicate states.

Transparent history

Search and filter every transaction by text, currency, status and date, and open a full detail view for any record.

Monthly statements

Per-currency statements with opening and closing balances, line previews and a simulated download.

Notifications and support

In-app notifications for account events and a support desk with tracked conversations.

Security controls

MFA settings, login history, active sessions with device details, suspicious-activity alerts and a temporary account freeze.

Deposits, simulated honestly

Four methods, five outcomes, one rule: only a successful simulated payment credits the balance — and only in the currency you deposited into.

Method Fee Availability Behaviour
Debit card 1.4% (min 0.50 currency units) All supported currencies Instant simulated debit-card charge.
Credit card 2.2% (min 1.00 currency units) USD, GBP, EUR, CHF Where supported — not available for NGN.
Payment gateway 1.0% gateway fee All supported currencies Simulated third-party checkout.
Bank transfer No fee All supported currencies Simulated transfer — settles as pending until confirmed.

The gateway is a simulation. Card payments run through a mock checkout with a pre-filled demonstration card; bank transfers show instructions and settle as pending. Outcomes can be successful, pending, failed, cancelled or duplicate — and the demo controls let you force each one. Pending deposits never credit a balance; failed and cancelled deposits never credit a balance; duplicates are rejected with a 409. No real money moves and no real provider is contacted.

Security and administrative transparency

Security features you operate yourself, and oversight of the people who run the platform — both visible in the prototype.

Verification and MFA

Email and phone one-time codes at registration, with an optional multi-factor setting you can toggle in Security.

History and sessions

Login history, active sessions with device, browser, IP and location — and one-click revocation of anything unfamiliar.

Alerts and freeze

Suspicious events are flagged with detail, and a temporary self-service freeze — with reason — can be applied and lifted instantly.

Audited takeover

When an administrator views an account, a permanent banner is shown, edits are logged, and every financial mutation is refused by the service layer.

What administrators can never do. Withdraw funds, transfer funds, edit balances, change deposit confirmations or modify historical ledger entries — for any role, at any time. The restriction is enforced in the service layer and appears in the audit log as a denial when attempted. Takeover sessions are not notified automatically; administrators can send a manual notification when appropriate.
