How it works

From registration to a working account

Five steps, in order. Each one is fully interactive in this prototype — the account you end up with holds per-currency balances, a complete transaction history and its own security controls.

The five steps

A considered sequence

1

Create your account

Register with your name, email, country and international phone number, and choose a password.

2

Verify email and phone

Enter the one-time codes sent to your email and phone. In this prototype the codes are simulated and shown on screen — nothing real is sent.

3

Your default currency activates

Based on your country — Nigeria gets NGN, the United States USD, the United Kingdom GBP, Switzerland CHF, eurozone countries EUR. It starts at a zero balance.

4

Add currencies and simulate deposits

Activate any of the five supported currencies. Each keeps its own balance, transaction history, deposit records and status — nothing is converted automatically.

5

Manage everything in one place

Track transactions, preview and download statements, manage security, sessions and notifications, and reach support.

A note on codes and payments

Everything sensitive is simulated

In this prototype the one-time verification codes are simulated and shown on screen — nothing is sent to a real email address or phone number. Deposits run through a mock payment gateway that can return successful, pending, failed, cancelled or duplicate outcomes. No real money moves and no payment provider is contacted.
