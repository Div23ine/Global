Closer reading · one

Multi-currency mechanics

How five currencies live side by side without ever touching each other.

Separate balances, by design

Each activated currency keeps its own balance, transaction history, deposit records and status. Activating a new currency never converts or moves funds from another — new accounts always start at zero, and the USD equivalents shown alongside balances are indicative only.

Country-based defaults

The currency for your country is activated automatically after verification: Nigeria → NGN, United States → USD, United Kingdom → GBP, Switzerland → CHF, eurozone countries → EUR, others default to USD. The mapping is configurable, and unsupported currencies can be requested from the activation screen.

Every deposit runs through a mock gateway that can return successful, pending, failed, cancelled or duplicate outcomes. Only a successful outcome credits the balance of the specific currency you deposited into — no real money moves.

Closer reading · two

Security, in depth

Four areas, each operable from the Security and Sessions sections of the application.

Verification and MFA

Email and phone verification with one-time codes at registration, optional multi-factor sign-in, and a password change that requires your current password and a confirmation.

Sessions and devices

Every active device listed with browser, operating system, IP address and location, your current device marked, and one-click revocation for anything you don’t recognise.

Freeze and alerts

Suspicious-activity alerts, a temporary self-service freeze with a stated reason, a recovery path through account verification, and a full security event history.

Audited administration

Administrators view accounts through a takeover mode with a permanent on-screen banner and a complete audit trail. Withdrawals, transfers, balance edits and deposit confirmations are blocked in the service layer — for every administrative role, without exception.
