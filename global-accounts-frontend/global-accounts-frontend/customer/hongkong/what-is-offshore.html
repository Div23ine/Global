What is an offshore account?

The plain-language version

!

Meridian provides platform accounts — not bank accounts, and not actual offshore banking services.

An international account from a provider like Meridian is a company-issued platform account you manage through our interface. In production, customer funds in platforms like this are held with regulated partner institutions and are subject to know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering checks in every market served.
