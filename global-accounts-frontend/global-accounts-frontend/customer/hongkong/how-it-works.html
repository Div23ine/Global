How it works

Five steps to a five-currency account

No paperwork queue in this prototype — registration, verification and activation all happen in the browser, exactly as the production flow is intended to work.

    A note on verification codes

    One-time codes are simulated in this prototype — the codes are shown on screen instead of being sent. Nothing real is delivered, and no real money is ever involved. In production, codes would arrive by email and SMS.
