Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · This notice follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Meridian Global Accounts operates this platform for customers in Europe. The operating legal entity and its registered address will be published with the commercial launch.

2. What we collect

Account data (name, email, phone, country), transaction and deposit records, statements, support messages, notifications, security events, and technical logs. Authentication is handled by our configured authentication provider; Meridian does not store plaintext passwords.

3. Why we process it

To provide and secure the service (contract), to meet legal and anti-fraud obligations (legal basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest), and to respond to support requests.

4. Processors we use

Identity management (Clerk), managed PostgreSQL hosting (Supabase), SMS delivery (Twilio), payment processing (licensed partners, per deposit method). Each processes data only on our instructions under a data-processing agreement.

5. Where data lives

In the European Economic Area to the extent practical; where a processor operates outside the EEA, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your GDPR rights

You may access, rectify, export and erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Requests can be made from your dashboard or to our support address. Erasure is honoured for personal data while financial records are retained only as long as law requires; retained records are pseudonymized.

7. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. On deletion, personal identifiers are anonymized; financial and audit records are retained under applicable retention rules in pseudonymized form.

8. Security

TLS in transit, encryption at rest, row-level security in the database, role-based administrative access, and an append-only audit log. No system is perfectly secure; we log and review security events.

9. Changes

We update this notice when our processing changes materially and notify you where required.
