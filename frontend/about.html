Who we are

Built for a world that moves across borders.

Meridian Global Accounts is creating a clearer digital experience for people who need to organise multiple currencies, follow funding activity and understand every account record.

Our approach

Clarity before complexity.

Cross-border financial activity can become fragmented across providers, currencies and records. Meridian brings supported currency views and account activity into one organised platform, with a focus on clear status information and traceable records.

We aim to make the experience understandable: what is available, what is pending, what has been confirmed and where each record came from.

  • One login for supported currency records.
  • Separate balances and histories for each currency.
  • Deposit progress presented with clear status.
  • Account activity organised for review and statements.

What guides the product

Principles that shape the experience

We focus on practical product principles rather than promises that cannot be demonstrated.

01

Transparency

Account activity should be understandable and traceable. Status changes should be visible rather than hidden behind vague labels.

02

Designed for multiple currencies

EUR, GBP, USD, CHF and PLN are presented as distinct currency accounts, each with its own balance and activity history.

03

Responsible partnerships

Money movement is handled through appropriately licensed financial partners supporting Meridian Global Accounts.

Important distinction. Meridian Global Accounts provides international multi-currency accounts and tools inside our application. The holding and movement of funds are subject to the arrangements and terms of the relevant licensed partners.
