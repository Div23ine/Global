About Meridian Global Accounts

Meridian Global Accounts builds technology for people who live, work and transact across borders. Our platform gives European customers one login for currency accounts in EUR, GBP, USD, CHF and PLN — with an audit trail they can actually read.

What we are — and are not. Meridian provides platform accounts: multi-currency records inside our application, with money movement executed exclusively through appropriately licensed financial partners. Meridian is not a bank and does not provide offshore banking services.

Transparency first

Every balance is a projection of an immutable ledger entry. Nothing is adjusted silently; every status change is attributable.

European by design

EUR is the default presentation currency. Dates, numbers and language formatting follow European conventions, and our roadmap is GDPR-driven.

Partnered money movement

We do not pretend to be a bank. Deposits and payments run through licensed partners; our job is the product experience and the audit trail.
