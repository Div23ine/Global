How Meridian works

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your email address and phone number. We send a one-time code to each to verify you are real. Password recovery and device management are handled by our identity provider, Clerk.

  2. 2

    Your default currency activates

    Based on the country you select at registration (Germany → EUR, United Kingdom → GBP, Poland → PLN, and so on), that currency's account opens automatically with a zero balance.

  3. 3

    Activate more currencies

    Add any of the supported currencies whenever you need them. Activation is instant, separate per currency, and never converts existing balances.

  4. 4

    Fund through a licensed partner

    Choose the currency, amount and payment method. Your deposit request is recorded and processed by our licensed payment partner. Nothing is credited until the partner confirms — you will never see a manufactured "success".

  5. 5

    Track everything

    Each currency has its own ledger: transactions, deposits, statements. Filter by currency, status or date; download statements on demand.

  6. 6

    Stay in control

    Review active devices, revoke sessions, and see security events. Administrative access is role-based, audited, and can never touch your balances.

One-way honesty rule. A deposit that is not yet confirmed by the payment provider shows as pending — not as success. Failed and cancelled deposits are never credited.
