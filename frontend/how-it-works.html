A simple account journey

From account setup to a clearer view of activity.

The Meridian experience is organised into a few understandable steps. Funding and money movement remain subject to the relevant licensed payment partner.

Your journey

Six steps to get oriented

What happens at each stage, and what to expect from your account.

01

Create your profile

Register with your email address, then complete the verification step presented during sign-up.

02

Default currency account

Your selected country determines the default currency account made available to you. It begins with a zero balance.

03

Activate other currencies

Choose from the supported currencies when you need another currency view. Activation does not exchange or move existing funds.

04

Submit a deposit request

Select the currency, enter the amount and follow the available instructions for the relevant payment method.

05

Wait for confirmation

The request remains pending until the payment partner confirms it. A pending request is not a credited balance.

06

Review your records

Use the transaction history and available statements to review activity for each currency account.

Our confirmation rule. A deposit is only reflected as credited after confirmation from the payment partner. Meridian does not manufacture successful payment states. Failed or cancelled requests are not credited.
