Default currency

Your registration country sets the starting point.

The country selected during registration determines which default currency account is activated. The platform currently maps the United Kingdom to GBP, Switzerland to CHF, Poland to PLN and the United States to USD. Many other listed European countries use EUR as their default.

You can activate other supported currencies later. This does not automatically convert your existing balances.

  • Default account starts with a zero balance.
  • Other supported currencies can be activated separately.
  • Each currency has its own transaction history.
  • Currency activation is not a currency exchange.
