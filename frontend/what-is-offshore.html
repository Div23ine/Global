What is an offshore account?

"Offshore account" usually means an account held in a country other than where you live. People use them to receive foreign income, hold a second currency, or keep money near their international obligations.

The traditional route

Opening an account with a bank in another jurisdiction — in person, with local documents, minimum balances and fees, and paperwork in another language.

The platform route

A digital service — like Meridian — that gives you multi-currency accounts online, with money movement handled by licensed partners in the background.

Important. Meridian provides platform accounts — not bank accounts, and not actual offshore banking services. Deposits are held and processed by appropriately licensed financial partners. Tax and legal obligations connected to holding foreign-currency balances depend on your country of residence; we recommend independent advice.
