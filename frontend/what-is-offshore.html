A plain-language guide

What does “offshore account” actually mean?

An offshore account is generally an account held in a country different from the account holder's country of residence. The exact legal and regulatory meaning depends on the provider and jurisdiction.

01

Why people consider them

People may need to receive foreign income, manage expenses in another country or maintain financial arrangements across borders.

02

What to check

Understand the institution, jurisdiction, fees, access rules, protections, tax reporting duties and the legal status of the account.

03

Stay compliant

Foreign accounts and balances may create reporting or tax obligations. These vary by residence and should be checked with a qualified adviser.
