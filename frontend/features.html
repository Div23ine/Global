Account features

◎

Multi-currency accounts

EUR, GBP, USD, CHF and PLN side by side. Independent balances, independent histories.

⚡

Instant activation

Activate a supported currency in seconds. New accounts start at zero — no automatic conversion.

⇄

Deposit requests

Card, bank transfer or gateway through a licensed partner, with end-to-end status tracking.

▦

Immutable ledger

Every transaction is an append-only ledger entry. Balances are always the sum of posted entries.

🗂

Statements

Generate a statement per currency account and period, preview it in-app and download it.

🔔

Notifications

Deposit confirmations, security events and support replies land in your in-app inbox.

🛡

Device sessions

See active sessions, revoke devices, and manage multi-factor sign-in from Security settings.

💬

Support tickets

Category-based tickets with threaded replies — no bouncing between channels.

⚙

Preferences

Email and push notification preferences, weekly summary, and a clean account settings view.

Administrative safeguards

Role-based access

Support, compliance and finance roles see only what their duties require. Every permission is checked on every request, server-side.

Financially read-only administration

Administrators can review records and edit permitted profile fields — never balances, transactions or deposit outcomes. Attempts are logged and refused by the service layer.
