The platform

The essentials, brought into one clear experience.

A focused set of tools for viewing supported currencies, following deposits and keeping a readable record of account activity.

Account management

Designed around the way you manage money records

Each feature has a clear role in the account journey, from activation through to ongoing review.

01

Multi-currency accounts

View EUR, GBP, USD, CHF and PLN separately under one login, with an independent balance and activity history for each.

02

Currency activation

Activate supported currency accounts when needed. Newly activated accounts begin with a zero balance; activation does not convert funds.

03

Deposit requests

Create a deposit request and follow its recorded status. Funds are not shown as credited until the payment partner confirms them.

04

Transaction history

Review account entries with filters that help organise activity by currency, status and date.

05

Statements

Generate and review statements for a selected currency account and period, where the account data is available.

06

Account notifications

Keep relevant account updates, deposit status changes and support replies together in the in-app notification area.

07

Session controls

Review active sessions and use the available security controls to revoke sessions you no longer recognise.

08

Customer support

Raise a support request and keep the conversation and replies associated with your account.

09

Personal preferences

Manage the notification and account preferences exposed in your settings.

Operational safeguards

Clear boundaries for administrative access.

Administrative access is designed around role-based permissions and auditability. Support access is for permitted operational purposes; it is not a mechanism for staff to alter customer balances or rewrite financial history.

  • Role-based access to customer records.
  • Administrative activity is logged.
  • Financial records are not editable through ordinary support access.
  • Deposit confirmation depends on the relevant payment partner.
