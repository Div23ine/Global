Before you send

A note about account security.

Please do not include passwords, verification codes, full payment card details or other sensitive financial information in a general enquiry.

  • For account-specific support, sign in first.
  • Never share a one-time verification code.
  • Include only the information needed to understand your general enquiry.

This public contact form is not connected to a message-delivery service yet. Submitting it will not send your enquiry.
