An international account platform, built for review

Meridian Global Accounts is a design prototype for a product people who earn, save and spend across borders actually need: money held in the currencies they use, managed from one place.

Our story (a demo one)

Meridian Global Accounts is a placeholder brand created for a frontend design prototype. The brief behind it is real: people who earn, save and spend across borders need a straightforward way to hold money in the currencies they actually use — without converting everything into a single local currency the moment it arrives.

The product concept is a platform account: one login, five currency balances (NGN, USD, GBP, EUR and CHF), each with its own history, deposits and statements. Your country determines the currency that activates first after verification; the rest are one click away. Nothing converts automatically, and nothing is hidden.

This prototype implements the complete customer experience — public site, registration with simulated verification, the dashboard, the deposit simulation, statements, security screens and an audited administrator console — so the design can be evaluated end-to-end before any backend is built.

What this prototype is

A frontend design concept for client review. Every user, balance, transaction and statement is demonstration data stored in your own browser.

Meridian does not provide bank accounts, actual offshore accounts, or regulated financial services. In production, customer funds in platforms like this would be held with regulated partner institutions.
