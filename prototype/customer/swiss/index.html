Features

What the account does

All features →

Multi-currency accounts

Hold NGN, USD, GBP, EUR and CHF side by side. Every currency account has its own balance, history and status.

Country-based activation

The currency for your country is activated automatically after verification. The mapping is configurable.

Flexible currency activation

Activate additional currencies yourself in seconds. New accounts always start at zero — balances are never auto-converted.

Simulated deposits

Debit card, credit card where supported, payment gateway and bank transfer — with success, pending, failed, cancelled and duplicate states.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

Full walkthrough →
1

Create your account

Register with your name, email, country and international phone number, and choose a password.

2

Verify email and phone

Enter the one-time codes sent to your email and phone. In this prototype the codes are simulated and shown on screen — nothing real is sent.

3

Your default currency activates

Based on your country — Nigeria gets NGN, the United States USD, the United Kingdom GBP, Switzerland CHF, eurozone countries EUR. It starts at a zero balance.

Security

Controls you can inspect

Verification, device-level session control and a transparent audit trail — security features you can open and operate, not fine print.

Verified access

Email and phone verification with one-time codes, plus optional multi-factor sign-in and a confirmed password change.

Session control

See every active device with browser, operating system, IP and location — and revoke anything you don’t recognise.

Activity you can audit

Login history and suspicious-activity alerts, recorded per event with timestamps and a full security history.

Temporary freeze

Freeze your own account instantly if something feels wrong, and unfreeze it just as fast once you are satisfied.

Administrative access is audited

Administrator view sessions show a permanent on-screen banner and write a complete audit trail. Withdrawals, transfers, balance edits and deposit confirmations are blocked in the service layer — for every administrative role, without exception.
