About

A study in banking discipline

Meridian Global Accounts is a placeholder brand created in 2021 for a frontend design study — an exploration of how an international multi-currency account platform might look, read and behave when it is designed with the restraint of a Swiss private bank.

The story

Founded 2021, built quietly

The project began with a narrow question: what would a digital account service look like if it were designed with the discipline of private banking rather than the urgency of a marketing department? Not the exclusivity — the discipline. White surfaces instead of decoration. Hairline structure instead of shadow. Numbers set like typography, not like dashboards.

Meridian is the answer as a working prototype. It serves customers in 24 countries across five supported currencies, with country-based default activation, per-currency balances and a transparent, auditable history. Every flow — registration, verification, deposits, statements, security, support — is fully interactive demonstration data.

The discipline is borrowed from Zurich; the service is deliberately global. Nigeria receives NGN by default, the United Kingdom GBP, Switzerland CHF, the eurozone EUR, and the remainder USD — a mapping configured per country rather than assumed. Nothing converts automatically, and every account starts at zero.

Values

Four commitments, kept quietly

The values are not posters on a wall; they are decisions you can verify on every screen of this prototype.

Clarity

Every screen states what it is and what it is not. Demo markers, sample labels and plain-language copy are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Discipline

Hairline borders, tabular numerals, one navy, two typefaces. The structure does the work that decoration usually has to fake.

Candour

Platform accounts are not bank accounts, and the interface says so — in the hero, in the FAQ, in the terms, everywhere.

Precision

Balances are computed as the sum of their ledger entries, so every figure is provably consistent with the history behind it.

Please note

What this prototype is

Meridian Global Accounts is one of three frontend design concepts built for client review. It is not a bank, does not issue bank accounts, and provides no actual offshore accounts or regulated financial services. Every user, balance, transaction, statement and notification is demonstration data stored in your browser only — the footer of every page carries the same notice.
