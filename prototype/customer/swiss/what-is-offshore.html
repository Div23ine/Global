What is an offshore account?

An ordinary, transparent financial tool

An international (often called “offshore”) account is simply an account held outside your country of residence, usually in a different currency. People use them to receive salaries abroad, hold savings in stable currencies, pay international tuition or suppliers, and keep money in the currency they actually spend.

In four parts

The substance, without the mystique

What it is

A multi-currency account that sits alongside your local bank accounts. You keep balances in several currencies and transact in each of them separately.

What it is not

It is not a replacement for your bank, and it is not a way to hide money. International accounts are ordinary, transparent financial tools used by millions of people.

Who uses them

Freelancers paid in USD or EUR, families funding education abroad, cross-border commuters, small importers and exporters, and savers who want currency diversification.
