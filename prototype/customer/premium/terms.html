Demonstration notice

This document is part of a frontend design prototype. It outlines the intended production terms for review purposes and is not a legally binding agreement.

Platform account, not a bank account

Meridian provides company-issued platform accounts for holding and managing supported currencies. These are not bank accounts. The platform does not provide actual offshore bank accounts or regulated banking services.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old, provide accurate identity and contact information, and complete email and phone verification. Additional identity verification applies in production under local know-your-customer rules.

Currency activation and balances

Your country determines the default currency activated after verification. You may activate additional supported currencies. Each currency account is separate: balances, history and deposits are held per currency and are never converted automatically.

Deposits

In this prototype all deposits are simulated. In production, deposits are accepted through licensed payment partners, are credited only after confirmation, and may be pending, failed, cancelled or rejected as duplicates depending on the payment provider response.

Administrative access

Administrators may access customer accounts through an audited takeover mode limited to viewing and permitted edits. Administrators cannot withdraw funds, transfer funds, edit balances, change deposit confirmations or modify historical ledger entries. Users are not automatically notified of takeover sessions but may receive manual notifications.

Liability

The prototype is provided “as is” for design evaluation, without warranty of any kind. Production terms on liability, indemnity and dispute resolution will be defined in the final agreement.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the product and its regulatory posture evolve. Material changes will be communicated through the platform in production.
