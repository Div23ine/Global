Currencies

Five currencies, held side by side

Meridian supports five currencies at launch. Each activated currency keeps its own separate balance, transaction history and deposit records — nothing is converted automatically, and activating a new currency always starts at zero. Availability is live in this demonstration: the Japanese Yen is currently shown in its unavailable state.
