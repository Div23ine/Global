Multi-currency accounts

Hold NGN, USD, GBP, EUR and CHF side by side. Every currency account has its own balance, history and status.

Country-based activation

The currency for your country is activated automatically after verification. The mapping is configurable.

Flexible currency activation

Activate additional currencies yourself in seconds. New accounts always start at zero — balances are never auto-converted.

Simulated deposits

Debit card, credit card where supported, payment gateway and bank transfer — with success, pending, failed, cancelled and duplicate states.

Up and running in minutes

Registration, verification, activation — three measured steps before your first simulated deposit.

The full walkthrough

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your name, email, country and international phone number, and choose a password.

  2. 2

    Verify email and phone

    Enter the one-time codes sent to your email and phone. In this prototype the codes are simulated and shown on screen — nothing real is sent.

  3. 3

    Your default currency activates

    Based on your country — Nigeria gets NGN, the United States USD, the United Kingdom GBP, Switzerland CHF, eurozone countries EUR. It starts at a zero balance.

Five supported currencies

Each activated currency keeps its own separate balance, history and deposit records — nothing is converted automatically. Availability is live in this demonstration.

Security you can operate

Verification, device-level session control and a transparent audit trail — features you can see and use, not fine print.

Verified access

Email and phone verification with one-time codes, plus optional multi-factor sign-in.

Session control

See every active device with IP and location, and revoke anything you don’t recognise.

Suspicious-activity alerts

Failed sign-ins and unusual patterns are flagged in your security history.

Temporary freeze

Freeze your own account instantly if something feels wrong, and unfreeze it just as fast.

Administrative access is audited and always visible. When an administrator views an account, a permanent banner is shown on every screen, permitted edits are recorded in the audit log, and withdrawals, transfers, balance edits and deposit alterations remain blocked — enforced in the service layer, not by hiding buttons.
