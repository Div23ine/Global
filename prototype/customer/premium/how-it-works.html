How it works

From sign-up to a working account

Five measured steps — registration, verification, activation, deposits and management. Each one is fully working in this prototype, and each one is honest about what is simulated.

The five steps

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your name, email, country and international phone number, and choose a password.

  2. 2

    Verify email and phone

    Enter the one-time codes sent to your email and phone. In this prototype the codes are simulated and shown on screen — nothing real is sent.

  3. 3

    Your default currency activates

    Based on your country — Nigeria gets NGN, the United States USD, the United Kingdom GBP, Switzerland CHF, eurozone countries EUR. It starts at a zero balance.

  4. 4

    Add currencies and simulate deposits

    Activate any of the five supported currencies. Each keeps its own balance, transaction history, deposit records and status — nothing is converted automatically.

  5. 5

    Manage everything in one place

    Track transactions, preview and download statements, manage security, sessions and notifications, and reach support.

What is simulated, plainly

Verification codes are simulated. The one-time codes for email and phone verification are generated locally and shown on screen in this prototype — nothing real is sent to any address or number. Deposits run through a mock payment gateway (a demonstration card number is pre-filled) and can return successful, pending, failed, cancelled or duplicate outcomes. No real money moves and no real payment provider is contacted, at any point.

Take the first step

Registration takes a few minutes and every subsequent step is on this page. The account you create exists only in your browser, for your review.
